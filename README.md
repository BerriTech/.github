---

## 🧪 What we're building

### 🎬 [Cine-AI](https://github.com/BerriTech/Cine-AI)
An AI-powered, **content-based** movie recommendation platform. No collaborative filtering, no "people also watched" — just TF-IDF + cosine similarity working through genres, plot, cast, director, and keywords to find what you'll actually love next.

`Python` · `FastAPI` · `Scikit-Learn` · `Pandas` · `Vanilla JS`

### 🕯️ [Descent in Shadows](https://github.com/BerriTech/Descent-In-Shadows)
A dark, psychological narrative game about guilt, suppressed memory, and confronting the self you've been trying to bury. Dynamic torch & sanity systems, branching endings, and an AI layer for context-aware dialogue on the roadmap.

`JavaScript` · `Canvas API` · `FastAPI` · `LLMs`
